EmisPass Verifiable embedded-emission passports for CBAM & TR-ETS
connecting…

A Turkish steel or cement exporter that cannot prove its real emissions pays CBAM on punitive default values. EmisPass turns a plant's monthly emission report into a passport that three independent AI verifiers must co-sign before it is anchored on-chain, and that an EU importer can check for its own shipment without seeing anyone else's data.

  1. Operator signsmonthly activity data & totals (EIP-712)
  2. Signed feedsgrid meter, fuel supplier, satellite
  3. 3 AI verifiersreconcile, recalculate, ML anomaly checks
  4. 2-of-3 on-chainpassport anchored, report hash bound
  5. EU importerMerkle proof, declares shipment once

      Trust boundaries

      • Operator can lie about activity data → caught by signed third-party feeds and independent recalculation.
      • One verifier can be bribed or hacked → k-of-n threshold (2-of-3), duplicate and unregistered signatures ignored.
      • Relayer can alter numbers in transit → EIP-712 signatures over every field, nonce blocks replay.
      • Importer could reuse a record → leaf bound to its address, declarable once.
      • AI model could be swapped → model hash of code, factors and ML config stored per passport.

      What stays off-chain

      • Plant process data and fuel invoices (only evidenceHash on-chain).
      • Full AI report (only reportHash, verifiable by anyone holding the file).
      • Per-shipment quantities and buyers (salted commitments in a Merkle tree).
